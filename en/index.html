Core principle

Context is not prediction.

The project teaches a repeatable process: observe, interpret, build conditional hypotheses, define invalidation and review what actually happened.

Observe

Regime, location, volatility, references, timing, flow and events.

Interpret

Acceptance, rejection, initiative, balance and failed continuation.

Plan

If/then scenarios, invalidation and no-trade conditions.

Review

Separate reading quality, execution quality and financial outcome.
