Routine

Seven-step pre-market preparation

Turn observation into a repeatable written process before the first trade.

1. Check the calendar

Mark relevant releases and times, and define how your method behaves around them.

2. Read the larger picture

Describe structure without forecasting.

3. Map references

Keep only levels that can change interpretation.

4. Classify regime and volatility

Trend, range, transition, compression or expansion — with room for uncertainty.

5. Write two or three scenarios

Use condition → interpretation → permitted behavior → invalidation.

6. Define a no-trade scenario

State when staying out is the correct decision.

7. Review before execution

Confirm that any trade would belong to a written scenario.

Post-market: review reading quality and execution separately from financial outcome.
